Install. Set. Close.

ClamDown is a menu bar app, so it stays out of the Dock and remains one click away.

Get started

  1. Download the latest ClamDown.dmg from GitHub Releases.
  2. Open the DMG and drag ClamDown into the Applications folder.
  3. Eject the ClamDown disk image, then open the app from Applications.
  4. Select the clam icon in the menu bar, enable Monitor lid closure, and choose your sleep delay.

On first launch, macOS may ask you to confirm that you want to open an app downloaded from the internet.

Requirements

Made for modern MacBooks

  • macOS 14 or later
  • A MacBook
  • An external display for automatic lid detection

Good to know

  • When the timer reaches zero, your Mac will actually sleep.
  • The panel may briefly reposition while macOS updates the display layout.
  • The countdown cannot remain visible if the external display turns off or disconnects.
